Mini HUD 0.4.7
2026-11-11

Recognizes exactly VTK OBSERVER_CONTRACT integer 1 with live structural checks.
The native Mini HUD final-blow witness and counters remain independent of VTK.
No VTK UI/dependency source hashes are required for contract v1. Unrelated VTK
changes can stay compatible while its agreed contract and live structure remain
intact. Unknown markers, death callbacks and signal listeners fail closed.
Keeps exact-source VTK 5.5.1, Lava 1.1.2 and AIAL 1.2.0 compatibility, with the
reviewed Metro 3.4.2 and 3.5.0 loader/wrapper families.

Baseline VTK is a local build of official 5.6.4 source at 59a544a, not a
verified released binary. Focused Metro 3.5.0 gameplay confirmed numeric player
kill counts with VTK HUD active, disabled mid-session and off before a fresh
launch; the tester also observed NPC-kill exclusion. Screenshots corroborate
counts and visible HUD states. Diagnostic traces were unavailable because of a
separate test-profile encoding defect; this is not trace-level attribution proof.
Both loader versions passed the contract and legacy modeled-combat matrix.

After the gameplay smoke, one v1-only guard was tightened to require valid
callbacks with the agreed 4/2/2 argument counts; negative fixtures cover this
change. The independent native witness, other runtime scripts, icons and
licenses remain unchanged. Final package verification uses the prepared build.
No changes to HUD appearance, shortcuts, settings, native damage or saves.
The previously recorded Lava-associated shutdown limitation remains: both new
gameplay exits matched the known crash signature. This release does not fix it
or certify arbitrary future VTK/game/loader versions or all companion features.

Mini HUD 0.4.6
2026-11-08

Fixes kill-counter N/A caused by generated-method section comments and the
reviewed Vostok Toolkit 5.5.1 and Murdie Lava Lamps 1.1.2 death callbacks.
Retains AI Actually Loots 1.2.0 support. Recognizes exact Metro 3.4.2 and
3.5.0 loader/wrapper sources. Other versions are not implied.

Preserves native player-final-blow attribution: NPC kills and repeat corpse
shots do not add credit. Other mods' kill totals and assist windows are not
used. Unknown or changed callbacks remain unsupported and fail closed to N/A.

Tested on game 0.2.3.1/build 26332724, Metro 3.4.2/3.5.0 and MCM 2.9.3.
Focused 3.4.2 player-first and NPC-first gameplay tests passed. A fresh 3.5.0
smoke confirmed seven player kills and no extra credit from corpse shots; no
NPC kills occurred in that run. Both loader versions passed 4,210 modeled-
combat regression assertions each, separately from gameplay evidence.
This does not certify arbitrary mod stacks, Total Kills/Tally,
or historical Punisher encounter attribution.

Known test-environment limitation: Lava Lamps 1.1.2 was associated with a crash
on normal game exit, including without Mini HUD. It can leave game logs
incomplete. Mini HUD alone and Toolkit alone exited cleanly. Cause and wider
reproducibility remain unconfirmed; this release does not fix that exit crash.

Mini HUD 0.4.5
2026-10-31

Compatibility update for Road to Vostok 0.2.3.0 and 0.2.3.1.
Updates the reviewed native AI/death compatibility checks so kill observation
can initialize on these builds. Preserves public 0.4.3's support for the exact
reviewed AI Actually Loots 1.2.0 death callback. UI, shortcuts, settings,
defaults and attribution behavior remain unchanged from public 0.4.3.
Unavailable or detected incomplete kill attribution still shows N/A.

Validation
- RTV 0.2.3.1 / Steam 26332724, Metro 3.4.2 and MCM 2.9.3: 632 passing
  native-engine assertions across AIAL enabled/disabled and developer mode
  on/off. Fixtures use modeled damage bodies and the real AIAL 1.2.0 handler.
- Focused human AIAL gameplay: two player kills, two verified AIAL callbacks,
  HUD counts 0 -> 1 -> 2, no HUD N/A or script errors in the played session.
  The live corpse-hit step was skipped; repeated corpse damage is covered
  by current actual-AIAL automated checks, not claimed as a new manual pass.
- Earlier focused 0.2.3.1 checks without AIAL covered trade/combat-close,
  HUD visibility, a player kill and corpse exclusion, Lore reading/journal,
  saved Cabin reload and Nomad/Lore persistence. Punisher stop/deployment
  passed, with two minions and boss activation confirmed in logs.
- RTV 0.2.3.0 / Steam 26303644: reviewed equivalent critical native contracts
  and earlier base-candidate engine fixtures; no completed human AIAL pass.

Known limits
The historical Punisher encounter kill undercount remains unresolved.
Stop/deployment validation does not establish Punisher kill-count accuracy.
Full normal mod stacks, MSS, broader maps, other AIAL versions, arbitrary
combat overrides and general AIAL item conservation were not revalidated.
Kill counts are not persisted between game launches. Tracing is off by default.

Package
Every runtime script, icon and license matches the accepted 0.4.3-rtv023.1
candidate exactly. Only the version metadata and this changelog changed.
No QA helpers, game source/assets, loader, other mods or saves are included.
Mini HUD code: MIT. Phosphor Icons: MIT; notices included.
